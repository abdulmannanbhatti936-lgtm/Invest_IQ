### Promotion decision (validation only)

Rule: promote the news model only if it beats the price-only model on BOTH validation LSTM RMSE and validation RF balanced accuracy. News model better on validation LSTM RMSE: yes; on validation RF balanced accuracy: no. **Do not promote the news model.**

### Does sentiment change a prediction?

On the 1707 test forecasts made on days with news, setting the news inputs to 'no news' changed the Random Forest signal on 37 and flipped the LSTM direction on 206; the LSTM forecast moved by 0.103 percentage points on average (at most 0.708).

Random Forest importance of the news inputs: news_weight 0.0091 (rank 17 of 17), sentiment 0.0117 (rank 16).
